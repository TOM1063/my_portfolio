Augmented Gyoza

Augmented Joy of Eating 🥟

Sensing the small movements at the table during our important mealtime and enhancing the experience.

made with TouchDesigner / Processing / Ableton Live

System 🔀

First, we created a system in Processing to track the positions of chopsticks using retroreflective material and an Intel RealSense D435.

Finally, we have created a system capable of detecting which kind of spice is moving !! GitHub
